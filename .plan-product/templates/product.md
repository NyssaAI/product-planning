# Product

## Purpose

Describe what the product is, who it serves, and why it exists.

## Audience

List the primary users and the jobs they need done.

## Problem

Describe the pain, gap, or workflow failure this product addresses.

## Solution

Describe the product shape and the smallest useful version.

## Boundaries

List non-goals, constraints, and things this product should not own.

## Success

List the observable outcomes that show the product is working.

## Features

The feature inventory, build order, and detailed feature guides are maintained in `docs/features.md` and `docs/features/`.

## Tech Stack

Runtime, framework, storage, tooling, and environment assumptions are maintained in `docs/tech-stack.md`.
