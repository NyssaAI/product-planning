# Concepts

- **Product:** the audience, problem, solution, boundaries, and intended outcomes recorded in the core documents.
- **Feature:** a capability with stable identity, explicit ownership, inputs, outputs, and acceptance checks.
- **Use case:** a concrete actor workflow within one feature, including its entry route, trigger, process, alternatives, and failures.
- **Roadmap slice:** a product deliverable with a stable `R-NNN` identity, one parent feature, optional dependencies, and an observable acceptance checkpoint.
- **Delivery order:** the order of roadmap slices; it may differ from feature phase numbering.
